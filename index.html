Operations command

Build a working intake block

Follow these steps to connect construction, intake, housing, meals, and staffing. With no tool selected, click a room or fixture to inspect its status and requirements.

  1. Pause while laying out the prison.

    Keep the clock paused during setup so arrivals and needs do not advance before the basics are ready.

  2. Hire a Workman first.

    Workmen carry materials and complete construction orders. Staff fees are charged when hired and wages continue each hour.

  3. Make a road-connected Delivery Yard.

    Mark at least 6×6 outdoor tiles, add a road gate on its edge, then place a Loading Pallet inside. The room stays invalid until all three are in place.

  4. Set up Reception for arriving prisoners.

    Mark a Reception room with a Search Table, Uniform Rack, and at least one seat or bench. Place those fixtures inside the room.

  5. Build secure housing.

    Enclose each Cell or Dormitory, put a Jail Door in its boundary, and place a Bed and Toilet inside. Use the room tool to designate the finished space.

  6. Prepare meals and cover the prison.

    Kitchen needs a Cooker, Freezer, and Sink. Canteen needs a Table and Bench; add a Serving Table for meal service. Hire a Cook and Guards for daily operations.

  7. Plan guard patrols.

    Draw a route across tiles, then assign an available guard to the beat. Blue and purple routes can cross without merging.

  8. Check room warnings, then resume.

    Open Problems to find missing room requirements or blocked construction. Once delivery, reception, housing, meals, and staff are ready, unpause the clock.
